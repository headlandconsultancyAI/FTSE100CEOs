Method and data notes

  • Follower and connection counts come from a separate LinkedIn profile export dated 2 July 2026, matched to each CEO by profile URL. All 54 CEOs are matched to a follower count. Most came from the profile export (matched by profile URL); António Simões and Zoe Yujnovich, absent or errored in that export, were added from counts supplied separately.
  • Follower counts are a blunt reach proxy: they mix long-built audiences with recent growth and say nothing about who those followers are. Read "Per 1k" as a directional efficiency signal, not a precise rate.
  • Source: 1,357 scraped LinkedIn posts across two exports. After removing reposts, company pages, and non-CEO staff swept in by the scraper, 817 original posts across the 54 ranked FTSE 100 chief executives were analysed.
  • Mean can be pulled up by one viral post, so median and best-post figures sit alongside it. Where the two diverge sharply, the mean is riding on a single strong post.
  • Julia Hoggett runs London Stock Exchange plc, the exchange business, rather than LSEG group, and is marked "label" on that basis.
  • CEOs whose only appearances were reposts or third-party mentions (no original posts of their own) do not appear, as there is nothing of theirs to score.
