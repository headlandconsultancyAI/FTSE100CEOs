Method and data notes

  • Source: a single LinkedIn export captured 19 August 2026 covering 1 May to 19 August. Using one capture keeps engagement counts consistent across the window.
  • Scope: original posts only (reposts removed), by the cleaned set of FTSE 100 CEOs. 44 posted original content in the window; 15 did not and are absent. The "Minimum 5 posts" view further removes CEOs whose ranking rests on fewer than five posts.
  • The short window makes means volatile, which is why the minimum-post filter is offered. Median and post count (n) sit alongside the mean so you can see how much each figure rests on.
  • Follower counts are from the profile export dated 2 July 2026 (António Simões and Zoe Yujnovich supplied separately) and are a blunt reach proxy, so treat "Per 1k" as directional. Julia Hoggett is marked "label": she leads London Stock Exchange plc, the exchange, rather than LSEG group.
